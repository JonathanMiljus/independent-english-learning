References

Every chapter is grounded in published linguistics, second language acquisition, and English grammar research. Sources are cited at the bottom of each chapter; the full list lives here.

  1. Biber, D., Johansson, S., Leech, G., Conrad, S., & Finegan, E. (1999). Longman grammar of spoken and written English. Longman.
  2. Butt, J., & Benjamin, C. (2011). A new reference grammar of modern Spanish (5th ed.). Routledge.
  3. Carter, R., & McCarthy, M. (2006). Cambridge grammar of English: A comprehensive guide. Cambridge University Press.
  4. Krashen, S. D. (1985). The input hypothesis: Issues and implications. Longman.
  5. Murphy, R. (2015). English grammar in use (4th ed.). Cambridge University Press.
  6. Swain, M. (1985). Communicative competence: Some roles of comprehensible input and comprehensible output in its development. In S. Gass & C. Madden (Eds.), Input in second language acquisition (pp. 235–253). Newbury House.
  7. Swan, M. (2016). Practical English usage (4th ed.). Oxford University Press.
  8. Swan, M., & Smith, B. (Eds.). (2001). Learner English: A teacher's guide to interference and other problems (2nd ed.). Cambridge University Press.
